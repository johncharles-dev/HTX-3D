04Method & fairness

  • Identical segmentation per engine. rembg conditions = u2net background removal on the raw photo; sam3 conditions = the cached SAM3 cutout cropped to bbox+10% then re-segmented — the same inputs the existing engines received. TRELLIS.2 consumes the pre-cut RGBA directly.
  • One uniform scorer. Every GLB (all 7 pipelines) scored by the current auto_scale (nvdiffrast silhouette + UniDepth-v2 depth) in a single pass.
  • Parity budget. TRELLIS.2 exported at 1024 texture / ~50k faces to match the existing benchmark meshes; it natively supports 4K / 1M-face output.
  • Note: figures are a fresh uniform re-score and supersede the June 2026 numbers (earlier, less-calibrated scorer). Single-photo depth caps even the best engine near ~20% MAPE — a scale limit, not a mesh-quality limit.
