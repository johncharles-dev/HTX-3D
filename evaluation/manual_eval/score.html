controls

·Drag to rotate · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. Each engine emits its model in its own orientation, so turn them to a comparable view before judging.
·Sync views keeps whatever alignment you set: line the models up by hand, switch sync on (nothing moves), then rotating any one turns them all by the same amount from their own pose.
·Hold Shift while dragging to re-adjust one model without moving the others.
·← → change object · R reset views · click the photo to enlarge. Scores save automatically in this browser.

geometry 1–5 · structure & proportion only, ignore colour

5All major structures present, correct proportions, thin parts (mirrors, antennas, arms, railings) intact
4Correct overall shape, minor detail loss
3Recognisable, but notable structures missing, merged, or blobby
2Distorted or partly collapsed; identifiable only from context
1Unrecognisable — flat sheet, blob, or wrong object

texture 1–5 · colour, markings, surface detail vs the input photo

5Colours and markings accurate, sharp, correctly placed
4Correct colours, some blur or smearing
3Approximate colours; markings smeared, stretched, or misplaced
2Mostly wrong colours, or heavy bleeding across surfaces
1No meaningful texture (flat grey / noise)

rules

1Score every slot, including the obviously bad ones — skipping biases the means.
2Judge against the input photo, not against the other renders.
3Rotate before judging geometry — the back of every object is hallucinated, so check it.
4First impression. Don't go back and even out earlier objects.
5Don't open blind_key.csv until you've exported.
