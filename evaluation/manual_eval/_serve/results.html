Image-to-3D engine evaluation

Four engines × two segmentation front-ends, on ten Singapore public-service objects. Measured on dimensional accuracy against manufacturer specifications, generation cost, and mesh integrity.

01What we found

Hunyuan3D · rembg is the weakest

Three independent measurements agree: highest dimensional error (35.2% vs 23.5–29.1%), 3× worse proportion accuracy than every other engine, 3 of 10 objects more than twice too flat, and the only engine whose geometry collapses to a flat sheet.

The top three are tied

No pipeline pair separates at p<0.05 on dimensional accuracy. Our measuring instrument (auto-scale, ~25% error) is as noisy as the differences between engines, so this axis cannot rank them.

They fail differently

TRELLIS 1 is fastest at 10 s. SAM 3D is topologically cleanest — 5 mesh components against TRELLIS.2's 185. TRELLIS.2 carries the most detail and the most even texture. Pick on the axis you need.

Cluttered scenes excluded

Four multi-object APICS scenes were removed: background removal cannot isolate the intended target there, so the result measures segmentation failure rather than engine quality, and their ground truth is estimated rather than published.

05The clearest failure

Every model was re-rendered from four angles. A collapsed mesh gives it away immediately: seen edge-on it is a line. Among the ten reported objects, the police motorcycle collapses under both Hunyuan modes and under no other pipeline — and the motorcycle has published manufacturer dimensions, so this is not an artefact of estimated ground truth.

07What we did not measure

  • No ground-truth 3D meshes exist for these objects, so Chamfer distance and F-score — the standard geometric metrics in this literature — are unavailable. Geometry is assessed indirectly. The fix is to run the same pipelines on a scanned dataset for comparable numbers.
  • Perceptual quality. A blinded human scoring pass is built and pending; visual-quality statements here are observational.
  • Sample size. 10 objects, single seed. Intervals are wide and the high-confidence column rests on 6 objects with published dimensions.
  • Four APICS scenes excluded. Multi-object photographs where background removal cannot isolate the intended target, and whose ground truth is estimated rather than published. Excluding them tightens every interval but also removes the two pairs that had reached p<0.05 — so the honest statement is that dimensional accuracy separates nothing on clean single-object inputs.
  • Four image metrics were dropped rather than reported: SSIM, PSNR and LPIPS measured against the source photograph came out near-identical for every pipeline — they were registering background and framing mismatch, not reconstruction quality. Watertightness was 0% everywhere. Reporting them would have implied precision we do not have.
